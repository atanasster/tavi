# Code of conduct

Tavi's community follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be respectful and constructive, and harassment of any kind is not tolerated, in
issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/atanasster). Reports are handled in
confidence, and the maintainers decide on and apply the consequences the
Covenant describes.
